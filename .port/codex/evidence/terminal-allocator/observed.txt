Interpretation:
  The Ghostty terminal constructor routes its first terminal allocation through
  the supplied allocator and returns OUT_OF_MEMORY when it rejects the request.
  Rustty never calls the supplied allocator, returns SUCCESS, and creates a
  terminal on Rust's global heap. This reproduces the implementation comments
  at crates/rustty-vt-c/src/terminal.rs:947-968 and the Box allocations at
  lines 1009 and 1062.
