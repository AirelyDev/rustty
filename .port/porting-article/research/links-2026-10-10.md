## Method

- **fact**: primary evidence first (R40). 76 facts link a Rustty commit whose message states the value, or a committed record at `58aa62a81`: `.port/phase-8/final/FINAL-VALIDATION.md`, the sanitizer logs in `.port/phase-8/final/` and `.port/phase-8/w9/`, `.port/phase-8/final/perf/ws10-suite-at-246f70287.txt`, `.port/ORACLE_SYNC.md`, `.port/phase-8/w10/`, `.port/parity-closure/UPSTREAM-DEFECTS.md`, `docs/ghostty-bug-fixes.md`, `.port/audits/GHOSTTY_LIFETIMES.tsv`, the `.port/tools/ci.sh` registry, the committed unsafe census JSONs. `plan_price` links the public Claude pricing page. Every other fact falls back to the research file at `RESEARCH_SHA`, anchored to the line that holds the value: the fact's own row in `refresh-2026-10-10.md` when there is one, otherwise a line holding the value (Fact-check section preferred, a keyword from the fact id required for short numbers, whole-field match in CSVs). About 40 anchors were checked or set by hand (PIN list in the generator); the rest were located automatically and spot-checked.
- **commit**: every 7 to 40 hex token in `content.mdx`, `src/components/journey/**` and the string values of `src/app/journey/data/*.json`, resolved with `git cat-file` in both repos. No token resolves in both. All Rustty hits are ancestors of `main`, all Ghostty hits ancestors of `origin/main`. Agents-vs-human tooltips account for most rows. CSS colour literals (`#d97934` and similar) and the float artifact `30000000000000004` are skipped. Two all-digit hashes (`949584651`, `486793623`) are included.
- **file**: key `<content.mdx line>:<text>` (current `src/app/journey/content.mdx` line numbers; Task 23 moves the route to `/porting`). Excerpt captions link the excerpt's lines at the cited commit, found by matching the excerpt text. Widget-text rows are keyed `<component file>:<line>:<text>`.
- **ref**: canonical URLs, checked with curl (`-L`, browser user agent) on 2026-10-10; the status is in `notes`. Rule texts link to their lines in the Rustty repo at `58aa62a81`.
- **widget**: the research dataset at `RESEARCH_SHA` (the `-2026-10-10` regeneration where one exists), or the primary ledger for `defect-map`.
