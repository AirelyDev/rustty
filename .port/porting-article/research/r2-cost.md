# Round 2: what the port cost a subscription user

Area: cost re-framing. The author paid flat monthly subscriptions, not per-token API bills. This dossier answers four questions: which plans were in effect, how much usage a maxed-out week of those plans represents, what the lost Mac Claude window (2026-06-21 to 2026-09-07) most likely consumed, and which models authored the commits.

Everything here is regenerated by one stdlib-only, read-only script:

```
python3 -I /Users/gael/projects/rustty/.port/journey-article/research/tmp/r2_cost.py
```

Inputs: `/Users/gael/projects/rustty-stats/data/{messages.csv.gz,prompts.csv}`, `~/.claude/projects/**/*.jsonl` (limit error messages), `~/.claude/history.jsonl` plus the VM copy in `rustty-stats/.cache/rvm/` (prompt text), and `git log HEAD` of `/Users/gael/projects/rustty` (HEAD 1d22ba80d). Outputs are `data/r2-cost-*.csv|json`. "MEASURED" below means "produced by that script" unless another command is given.

Timestamps are UTC. "Week" means a Monday-start UTC week unless it says "quota week". A quota week is the subscription's own 7-day window, which runs from Tuesday 22:00 UTC (3pm Pacific) to the next Tuesday 22:00 UTC.

**Caveat on the ceiling.** The subscription meters usage in its own units, and API list price is only a proxy for them. The proxy varies by model. Week 09-22 ran mostly on Opus 5.5, whose list price is 20% lower for input and output and 60% lower for cache reads than Opus 5. That week used 2.5× the tokens of the weekly-limit weeks. The spread from $1,974 to $4,262 is therefore the honest uncertainty.

The ceiling is also a lower bound on the true capacity of the plan, for two reasons. Usage from claude.ai/code cloud sessions and from Claude chat apps counts against the same quota but leaves no token data. Extra-usage credits may also have extended some weeks.

**Rustty's measured share of a maxed subscription.** Over the four complete quota weeks, Rustty is **53.9%** of all Mac Claude API-equivalent spend ($6,539 of $12,132; MEASURED). That independently supports the author's "about half".

## 4. Extrapolating the lost Mac Claude window (06-21 to 09-07)

**Method.** The author's assumption is applied literally: in every week of the lost window with any Rustty activity, the Claude subscription was maxed out, and half of it went to Rustty.

- A week is active if it has a Rustty prompt on the Mac or VM, or a non-merge commit.
- Each active week is credited with ceiling × 0.5 × (lost days ÷ 7).
- The Claude usage already recovered in those days (VM transcripts; Mac remnants on 08-18 and 09-01) is part of the same subscription, so it is subtracted and not double-counted.
- Week 08-03 had no Rustty prompts and no commits, so it counts as zero.
- The window covers 10 full active weeks, plus 1 day (06-21) and the lost portion of week 08-31.

Per-week values are in `data/r2-cost-weekly.csv`; chart-ready long format is in `data/r2-cost-weekly-chart.csv`.

| scenario | ceiling | lost Mac Claude, API-equivalent, Rustty share | provenance |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| low | $1,974/wk | **$8.7k** | ESTIMATED |
| central | $2,769/wk | **$12.8k** | ESTIMATED |
| high | $4,262/wk | **$20.3k** | ESTIMATED |
| sensitivity: central ceiling, per-week Mac prompt share instead of a flat 50% | | $10.1k | ESTIMATED |

**Cross-checks** (ESTIMATED; these use different proxies, so they bracket the result rather than validate it):
- **Commits × recovered $/commit** (round 1, corrected): ~$6.8k. This is the lowest, because early commits were cheaper per commit than the long orchestrator runs of September.
- **Lost-window Mac Rustty prompts × recovered $/prompt:** 511 × $33.9 = ~$17.3k.
- The subscription-ceiling central of $12.8k sits between the two.

**Where 50% is generous and where it is not.**
- In the quiet weeks 08-10 and 08-17, Rustty had only 12 and 15 Mac prompts, against about 190 prompts on other projects. That is a 6-8% prompt share, so a flat 50% overstates those weeks by roughly $2.4k (central).
- In weeks 07-06 and 07-27, Rustty's prompt share was 70% and 86%, so 50% understates them.
- The prompt-share sensitivity row nets these out, at $10.1k.
- The quiet stretch is consistent with the author's account: work moved to other projects when limits bit. Codex in 07-19 to 07-25 and 08-03 to 08-17 was spent almost entirely on other projects (`sessions.csv`).
